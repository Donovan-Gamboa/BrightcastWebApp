OFFICIAL RULES

🏆 How to Win

  • Diversity: 5 Spellcasters with different names in play.
  • Unity: 5 Spellcasters with the same name in play.

First to win 2 games wins the match!

⚔️ Turn Order

  1. Draw Phase: Draw 1 card at start of turn.
  2. Action Phase: Play 1 card & use its ability.
    • Spellcasters stay in play.
    • Monsters (Dragon) are discarded.
Optional Skip: Instead of playing a card, draw 1 extra card and end turn.
Hand Limit: Max 8 cards. Discard excess at end of turn.

⚡ Interrupts (The "Stop")

Stop an opponent's card as it's played!

Cost: Discard 1 Wizard + 1 Matching Copy of their card.
Result: Their card is negated and discarded.

🃏 Card Abilities

DRUID (Spellcaster): Look at opponent's hand, force them to discard 1 card of your choice.

SAGE (Spellcaster): Draw 2 cards, then discard 1 card from your hand.

WARLOCK (Spellcaster): Return 1 Spellcaster from your discard pile to your hand. (No Monsters/Wildcards).

SORCERER (Spellcaster): Destroy 1 of your opponent's cards in play.

WIZARD (Spellcaster): Draw 1 card. (Or hold in hand to use as an Interrupt).

DRAGON (Monster): Destroy up to 3 of your opponent's cards in play.

ALCHEMIST (Wildcard): Exact copy of a Spellcaster you already have in play.
